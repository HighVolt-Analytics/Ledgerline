Controls

Six gates between the invoice and the wire.

  • Duplicate preventionThe same invoice cannot be paid twice, however it arrived.
  • Approved-only paymentNothing can be paid until the right person has said yes.
  • Three-way matchNo payment without an order and proof the goods arrived.
  • Multi-tier approvalLarger amounts climb to the people who own them.
  • Segregation of dutiesThe person who enters a bill cannot also release it.
  • Append-only auditNothing can be edited away after the fact.

Enforced server-side. The interface may hint; the API refuses. Funds never leave your bank — we instruct, your bank executes.
